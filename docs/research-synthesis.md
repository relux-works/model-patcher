# Research synthesis

**9 November 2026.** This summarizes design and public-source studies completed on 7–8 November. They establish requirements, alternatives and conditional calculations. They supply no independently measured patch improvement, qualified application route or demonstrated monthly evaluation capacity.

**Supported** identifies a narrow fact documented by a linked primary source. Experimental results from outside this project remain **author-reported**. **Inferred** identifies a design recommendation. **Illustrative** identifies invented outcomes or assumed resource inputs. **Unknown** identifies an unresolved property. Inspecting documentation and checking arithmetic are separate from running a model or reproducing a result.

## Findings and their limits

| Study | Finding carried forward | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog requirements and alternatives | Preserve publisher/model browsing and machine readability; distinguish fix, artifact, run and decision identities | A proven best file layout, contribution cost or demand for a new CLI |
| Benchmark selection and economics | Match the task to the claimed fix; compare complete paired jobs per dollar | A universal quality score, hardware winner or hosted-service capacity |
| Full-benchmark capacity | Count complete populations, distinct treatment arms and rounded paid sessions | Measured throughput or capacity from hypothetical token lengths and prices |
| Qwen3.8-27B GPU studies | Published short-output results concern distinct artifacts and workloads | Native/quantized equivalence, long-thinking performance or exact patch compatibility |
| Evidence tiers and quantization-aware discovery | Preserve paired outcomes, negatives, uncertainty, refusal reasons and exact consumer profiles | A validated statistical implementation or adopted record schema |
| Cross-profile and runtime-treatment addendum | Represent native-relative loss, gains in both profiles and active adapter stacks | Empirical order effects, runtime support or transfer of native gains |
| MacBook Pro nighttime study | Memory, complete-job duration and resumability constrain usable work | Overnight capacity inferred from a short decode test or nominal machine-hours |
| Free and sponsored compute study | Demo grants, notebook quotas and paid credits have different uses and constraints | Guaranteed free GPU CI, an award, eligibility or available hardware |
| Monthly-budget correction | **Owner decision, 8 November 2026: $30 total per month, with no separate daily GPU-hour cap** | An obligation to spend, a 30-hour ceiling or permission to provision resources |

The earlier one-hour/day and 30-hour/month assumptions are obsolete. Earlier general hardware recommendations also narrow to their original workloads. No model download, evaluation, rental, provider application or account setup was performed for this synthesis.

## Recommended direction

**Inferred:** start with a small machine-readable catalog and immutable evidence bundles, accompanied by Markdown explanations under publisher/model browsing levels. Keep each score in its run record and derive discovery from all registered evidence. This refines the earlier per-fix README/JSON proposal to accommodate shared baselines and conflicting profile-specific results. A generated index, service, migration framework and new applicator are not prerequisites.

[Catalog and evidence design](catalog-and-evidence.md) records the proposed meanings, alternatives and correction policy. [Evaluation design](evaluation-design.md) distinguishes smoke checks, finite-test descriptions and inferential claims. [Compute constraints](compute-constraints.md) separates published observations from resource scenarios. The [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) orders the work and its acceptance checks.

Reuse remains the starting point. [Hugging Face model cards](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/en/model-cards) provide Markdown with structured metadata, and [Hub evaluation results](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/eval-results) provide result discovery and display. **Inference:** these are useful vocabulary and publication surfaces, but the inspected fields alone do not supply this project's entire ordered-treatment, paired-outcome and correction-history contract. Whether upstream records can meet the initial user's needs with a smaller addition remains an open decision.

The existing [targeted-edit example](research-status.md), [reuse routes](reuse-routes.md) and [combination evidence](combination-evidence.md) retain their own source bindings and limitations. Neither a valid record nor a verified output hash establishes useful behavior. No public example below represents a supported fix.

## Decisions still needed

Choose the first concrete user problem, exact base and consumer profile, fix source and permitted application method. Confirm rights to the artifact and evidence, including dataset and generated-output retention. This repository's license remains an owner decision. Select intended-use guardrails and practical margins before evaluating a candidate. Establish measured memory and whole-job costs before promising capacity.

The source studies compared fictional cases and public documentation; they did not measure user demand or implementation maintenance. A catalog that links adequate upstream evidence may be sufficient. A CLI or hosted evaluator remains conditional on a demonstrated recurring gap.
