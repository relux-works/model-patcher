# Contributing evidence and corrections

This project is research only. Contributions that improve the accuracy of a claim, identify a limitation, or document a negative or inconclusive result are welcome. Link to primary sources and distinguish what they report from your interpretation. Include a stable revision or access date where possible; explain inaccessible material and corrections to earlier claims.

For a proposed fix or combination, identify the exact base model and files, source and revision, quantization, hashes and what each hash covers, and applicable licenses. Identify each fix by source and immutable revision, the order used, the method and version, and any intermediate and final file identities available. Describe prerequisites, input mismatches, overlapping edits, and observed conflicts. If a detail is missing, say it is unknown. A hash can identify a file but cannot establish that it is safe, effective, or compatible.

For evaluation evidence, include the original baseline, relevant individual fixes, and each tested ordered combination under comparable conditions. Identify the dataset revision and split, example count, prompt and chat template, tokenizer, runtime and version, decoding settings and seeds, metric and scoring procedure. Link configurations and per-example outputs where sharing is lawful and appropriate; otherwise explain the restriction. Report failures, truncations, regressions, sample and repeat counts, uncertainty and differences in conditions. Explain what was not evaluated. If a negative result or input incompatibility is observed, include the evidence and context; do not label an untested case incompatible.

A successful application or load check is separate from a quality comparison. Benefits on a benchmark do not establish benefits on every workload, and a positive pair does not establish compatibility with a third fix. Do not sum single-fix improvements to predict a combination. If reproducing an author-reported result, say whether the result is independently observed or quoted and preserve the original limitations.

For a correction, point to the exact passage, explain the issue, and provide a supporting source or reproducible observation. Do not publish restricted model files, private data, or material you lack permission to share. Suggestions may remain open research records when evidence is incomplete; they should not be described as supported fixes until a specific path and its claims are validated.
